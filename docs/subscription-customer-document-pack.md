   settle the remaining C01 facts without treating Home office as a buyer.

2. **Match the approved transaction.** Reconcile the final amount/currency,
   customer/organization, quote, immutable offer/policy, original payment and
   document treatment. Issue timing must match the reviewed actual transaction.
   No template opens a Production gate or replaces rollout approval.
3. **Verify each document's claim.** Invoice dates and payment status must be
   factual. A receipt requires captured-money proof; paid access requires its
   committed result. A refund confirmation requires exact full processed facts
   and committed access termination. Never use synthetic staging facts.

   unique receipt/refund references only at approved issuance. Save the final

   delivery method before sending anything. This packet provides no send
   authorization. SaaS documents remain separate from the gym's member-invoice
   ledger and document feature.

   mismatch or changed supplier/tax fact pauses the affected document. Record an
   owner, status, evidence and next action; do not manufacture a successful
   receipt or retry an uncertain provider POST to complete the packet.
